People sharing a room

Meet sees accounts, not everyone sitting together. Replace a shared account with the people who will take a turn.

Reading your meeting…

Include the account owner if they are present. These names replace the account’s single entry.

Suggest names from Google Calendar

Connect to read events on your primary calendar near the current time and match their Meet link. Invitees are suggestions: select only people actually in the room.

After saving, return to your Meet tab and reopen the extension. Room choices last for this browser session, up to six hours. Refresh preserves them; Reset removes them. People whose account leaves the call are omitted. “Add people without replacing an account” entries stay until you reset them.
